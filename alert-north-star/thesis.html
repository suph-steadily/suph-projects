Step 2 · Flip the switch

What we expect when the alert goes dark

The upside
  • +115 bound policies / month (range +50 to +175)
  • 2,200 desk reviews / month eliminated
  • $310K / yr of UW premium corrections kept — automated, not lost
What must hold
  • NOCs: ±0 — raw removal would add ~25/mo (4.5 → 6.5 per 100)
  • UW corrective endorsements: ±0 — raw removal would add ~30/mo (3.9 → 7.5 per 100)
  • Loss ratio: unknown — the weight the machine still can't price

Why ±0 is credible: the un-gated twin gets about 2 extra NOCs per 100 — and roughly 1.5 of those are things the four jobs cover (roof, eligibility, data). The last half comes from what the on-site inspection finds, which the review doesn't prevent today either. The honest caveat: without the jobs, the true un-reviewed rate projects to 7–11 per 100, because the 101+ book averages 117 years old and risk ramps +22.8% per decade.

Step 3 · The honest part

The residual doesn't go away — and it was never the alert's

Even with an underwriter reviewing every single bind, the reviewed book still runs 4.5 NOCs and 3.9 corrective fixes per 100. Duplicating the underwriter keeps that rate. Here's why the review can't see it coming:

This is the next frontier, not a reason to keep the alert. ~60% of residual NOCs are general-condition findings, ~20% liability hazards (trampolines, animals, structures). Shrinking them means moving condition signal pre-bind: imagery at quote time, attestation questions. Worth doing whether or not the alert lives.

One exception proves the roof lever: of the 12 roof-condition NOCs on the reviewed book, zero had the roof exclusion at bind. The one lane the review could have caught, it skipped — and the dial fixes exactly that.

Step 4 · Sequence

Weigh first. Test maybe never.

  1. Build the weighing machine (with David Curry).

    Nobody has appetite for "pick 7 states and eat the NOCs." Sizing the risk on paper replaces discovery-by-test. Inputs: bind LTV, NOC cost, UW premium per alert.

  2. Get LaNae's tolerance — in a named unit.

    74% of NOCs cure, so "letters sent" and "policies lost" differ 4x. Proposal: uncured cancellations per 100 binds, 90 days.

  3. Automate the four jobs until the sized risk beats the tolerance.

    Roof dial and pre-fill sourcing are ready to start; attestations next; Spotlight signals ~October.

  4. Turn it off for 10%. Watch. Then all of it.

    Automation only defends the NOC rate — the 63 who die at the roadblock come back only when the alert itself disappears. The canary slice proves the systems truly carry over before the growth switch is thrown for everyone.
