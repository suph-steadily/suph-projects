The idea

Automate those four jobs so the system does exactly what the underwriter does. Then turn the alert off.

Plain words: a NOC is a cancellation letter. An NOE is a coverage change letter.

The distinction

Automation defends. Removal unlocks.

Automating the underwriter's jobs only protects the NOC rate. It recovers none of the 63 who die at the roadblock. They come back only when the alert itself disappears.

Automation is the hedge. Removal is the growth.

A repeatable loop. Dwelling age is the first alert through it; every later one is cheaper.

2

4

6
